# Vertical and context adjustments

Pick the profile closest to the brand and apply its emphasis. The workflow in SKILL.md stays the same; the weights change.

## Contents

- Local and service businesses
- Agencies and professional services

- Non-US and multilingual markets

## B2B SaaS and developer tools
- Highest-leverage: comparison and alternatives pages, pricing transparency, G2/Capterra/TrustRadius presence, integration pages, public docs, original benchmark data, founder-led content.
- Agent readiness: clean docs, OpenAPI spec, MCP server or integrations listing, clear self-serve path. Assistants increasingly evaluate and act on tools, not just cite them.
- Watch: competitor "vs" pages that frame you unfairly; fix with your own factual comparison and corrections on the source.

## Ecommerce and DTC
- Highest-leverage: Product schema, specs completeness, reviews with volume and recency, buying guides, category "best for" pages, return/shipping policy clarity, marketplace and retailer listings, YouTube reviews.
- Keep price, availability and variants consistent between site, feed and marketplaces. Mismatches get an item dropped or misquoted.

## Local and service businesses
- Highest-leverage: Google Business Profile, reviews and owner replies, consistent NAP, local pages with services and areas, local directories, Apple Business Connect and Bing Places, community and press mentions.
- Prompts are location-bound ("near me", neighborhood, city). Audit with the actual city and device location settings.

## Agencies and professional services
- Highest-leverage: named expert profiles, case studies with metrics and permission, specialization pages ("[service] for [industry]"), speaking and podcast presence, awards and directories (Clutch, industry bodies), original POV content.
- Position narrowly; assistants recommend specialists for specific asks.

## Publishers and media
- Highest-leverage: original reporting and data, clear bylines, dated updates, licensing stance (decide deliberately on training vs search access), structured article markup, topic hubs, fast pages.
- Measure referral vs. zero-click effects; consider licensing programs where offered.

## Personal brands and founders
- Entity first: personal site with Person schema, consistent bio, linked profiles (`sameAs`), a clear one-line description of who you are and what you are known for, notable work and press, podcast and talk listings.
- Wikipedia/Wikidata only if notability is genuinely met; do not self-promote there.

## Regulated and YMYL (health, finance, legal)
- Expert authorship and review with credentials, citations to primary and official sources, up-to-date dates, clear disclaimers, no unsupported claims. Assistants are conservative and cite authorities; thin or promotional content is excluded.
- Do not generate medical, legal or financial claims for the brand; require the owner and a qualified reviewer to supply and approve them.

## Non-US and multilingual markets
- Test prompts in the languages and scripts real buyers use (including code-mixed text such as Hinglish) and with local phrasing, currencies and regulations.
- Localize pages properly (not machine-translated filler), use hreflang, keep entity facts identical across languages, and seek local sources: regional publications, directories, marketplaces, forums.
- Engines differ by country in availability and index; verify which assistants and search surfaces matter in the target market before allocating effort.
